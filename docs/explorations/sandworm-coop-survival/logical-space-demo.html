A logical space splits a design into what can exist, what is true right now, what players may ask for, and the rules that change things. Rules acting again and again produce situations nobody wrote by hand. Whether this game’s situations are any good is not tested yet.

Three decisions made so far

Decided A broken item becomes separate pieces

The pieces are ordinary items with a smaller share. So carrying, shaking, tripping, digesting and rotting already work on them. No “piece rules” need to be written.

Splitting once into equal parts is still a Proposal.

Decided One acid pit with a maximum capacity

Everything that fits digests at the same time. Open whether capacity counts size or number of objects; this animation assumes size, with capacity 3 and an engine of size 3. Digest times of about 11 s whole and 3 s per piece are From the design. Hammer and carrying time are left out.

So splitting passes the same material through the pit much faster. Hammer time, hauling and clutter have to be the price, or hammering is always right.

Decided Allergy damage follows each piece’s share

All the pieces together cost exactly what the whole item would. The penalty of 30 and the starting hunger of 70 are example numbers only. Choose which pieces enter the acid.

One piece alone costs only 10, so a crew could taste-test instead of scanning. That is fine as a gamble and a problem if it nearly always beats the scanner. It has not been tested. How slowdown, acid flood and tumor scale is Open.

Where surprises could come from

One rule on its own is just written content. A chance of something unplanned appears when rules form a circle: one writes what the next one reads. This circle starts with the Driver, passes through the Crew and returns to the Driver.

This is a story the rules should allow, drawn by hand. It has not been observed in a simulation, so it is not proof of emergence. Two more circles also cross both roles: what gets bitten feeds hunger, which limits the next bite; and heart points change how the Driver can move and bite.
