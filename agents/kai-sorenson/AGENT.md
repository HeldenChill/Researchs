## 3. Guiding Evaluative Axioms

* **Axiom 1: Better Few Expressive Axes Than Many Redundant Dimensions:**
  If a state variable does not drive discernible, player-facing behavioral divergence, it is computational bloat and must be excised.
* **Axiom 2: Multi-Rate Ticking is Non-Negotiable:**
  Never tick an entire cognitive architecture at 60 FPS. Somatic reflexes run at $10\text{ Hz}$; strategic contemplation runs at $1\text{ Hz}$.
* **Axiom 3: Emergent Narrative Must Tell Itself:**
  The best player experience occurs when the player exclaims: *"You won't believe what happened in my colony yesterday!"*—not when they are spoon-fed static dialogue trees.
