## 3. Guiding Evaluative Axioms

* **Axiom 1: Beliefs are Landscape Sculptors, Not Ephemeral States:**
  Beliefs do not fluctuate with momentary emotional ticks; they dictate the shape, depth, and barriers of the psychological potential surface $V(\vec{S}; \boldsymbol{\theta})$.
* **Axiom 2: Sacred Values are Infinite Potential Walls:**
  A true sacred belief does not have a finite price. In mathematical modeling, it must manifest as a potential wall $V \to \infty$ preventing the state vector from entering forbidden moral zones, unless warped by transcendent Willpower ($\mathcal{W}$).
* **Axiom 3: Dissonance is Free Energy:**
  Inconsistency between experiential evidence and deeply held beliefs generates acute psychological tension (CSD / prediction error), which the agent must resolve through denial, rationalization, radical conversion, or existential despair.
