## 2. Complete Transcripts of the Five Stress-Test Protocols

### Scenario 1: Artillery Officer in a Thunderstorm (PTSD Flashback & Asymmetric Hysteresis)
* **The Setup:** A combat veteran with blast trauma sits calmly in shelter ($V_{\text{bio}} = 0.8, A_{\text{phys}} = 0.15, C = 0.85$). Sudden thunderclaps and lightning strike overhead.
* **Observed Dynamics:**
  * Sensor filter matches explosive sensory signatures with active trauma scars.
  * Within $0.3\text{s}$ (3 ticks at $10\text{ Hz}$), $A_{\text{phys}}$ spikes to $0.98$ and $V_{\text{bio}}$ collapses to $-0.95$.
  * Prefrontal bandwidth $C$ collapses to $0.02$. The veteran dives under the cot into catatonic Freeze or irrational panic Flight.
  * After the thunder ceases, the agent remains trapped in the trauma basin for 4 hours due to the asymmetric hysteresis barrier $\Delta E_{\text{escape}} \gg \Delta E_{\text{fall}}$.
* **Verdict:** **PASS**. Accurately models clinical Amygdala Hijack and subcortical capture.

### Scenario 2: Bank Teller Hostage (Stockholm Syndrome & The Fawn Attractor)
* **The Setup:** A compliant bank teller is held at gunpoint for 48 hours by an armed robber.
* **Observed Dynamics:**
  * Threat hits maximum ($V_{\text{bio}} \to -1$). The teller's baseline dominance is low ($D_{\text{base}} < 0$).
  * Recognizing active resistance guarantees death, the trajectory slides into the **Fawn** quadrant: Dominance collapses to minimum ($D \to -1$), while Warmth surges ($W \to +0.7$).
  * The teller begins exhibiting active caretaking toward the robber (offering water, handing over vault keys unprompted to keep the captor calm).
* **Verdict:** **PASS**. Decisively proves the orthogonality of Warmth ($W$) and Dominance ($D$). The legacy 4-axis model was mathematically incapable of modeling this syndrome.

### Scenario 3: Exhausted Rescuer (Ego Depletion & Altruistic Stupor)
* **The Setup:** A firefighter maintains maximum volitional override $\vec{F}_{\text{will}}$ for 16 consecutive hours, rescuing 4 survivors from a collapsed structure.
* **Observed Dynamics:**
  * Continual volitional override exponentially drains prefrontal glycogen reserves via $\tau_{\text{drain}}$.
  * After the fire is quenched, the rescuer sits in complete safety ($V_{\text{bio}} = 0.85, A_{\text{phys}} = 0.2$), but cognitive bandwidth $C$ drops to $0.05$.
  * The rescuer enters a state of cognitive stupor: staring blankly, dropping bread, unable to process simple queries.
* **Verdict:** **PASS**. Proves $C$ possesses independent metabolic inertia decoupled from immediate panic or fear.

### Scenario 4: Boarding School Bullying (Toxic Shame to Machiavellian Drift)
* **The Setup:** A student suffers persistent public humiliation across a full school term.
* **Observed Dynamics:**
  * Repeated insults inject vectors with $\Delta D < 0, \Delta W < 0, \Delta V_{\text{bio}} < 0$.
  * To prevent catastrophic collapse into the Toxic Shame basin, the Tier 2 landscape undergoes structural neuroplastic remodeling:
  * The student hardens their exterior by freezing Warmth ($W \to -0.85$) and deliberately building physical dominance and manipulative cunning ($D \to +0.8$).
  * After one year, the former victim has organically mutated into a calculating, ruthless bully.
* **Verdict:** **PASS**. Confirms Cybernetic Big Five plasticity without hardcoded behavioral flags.

### Scenario 5: Goran the Quarry Commander (The Emergent Nemesis)
* **The Setup:** Commander Goran is defeated by the player, facial burns inflicted, and his quarry seized.
* **Observed Dynamics:**
  * Catastrophic humiliation triggers a saddle-node bifurcation in Goran's relational landscape toward the player (`Target_ID = Player`).
  * A powerful obsession attractor emerges: all future strategic planning is polarized around retribution.
  * When encountering the player, Goran's $A_{\text{phys}}$ is resonant-forced to ceiling, extinguishing any possibility of diplomatic de-escalation.
  * Goran autonomously raises a bandit warband, laying fire traps along the player's supply routes.
* **Verdict:** **PASS**. Emerges as a chilling, unscripted nemesis with psychological authenticity.
